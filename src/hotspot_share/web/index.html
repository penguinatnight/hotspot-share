Overview

Hotspot Share is a zero-cloud, privacy-first peer-to-peer file sharing and synchronization suite designed for Linux desktops and smartphones. Transfer large files and directories at full Wi-Fi link speeds via a direct 8MB chunked transfer engine, and seamlessly sync clipboard text and images between your devices—with zero cloud telemetry, zero account logins, and zero phone apps to install.
